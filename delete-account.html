Account Management

Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your AbikCare account and personal data at any time. Follow the steps below.

⚠️

This action is permanent. Once your account is deleted, your profile, order history, and personal data cannot be recovered. Please read the information below before proceeding.

How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account directly from within the AbikCare mobile app:

1
Open the AbikCare app

Launch the app on your Android or iOS device and sign in to your account.

2
Go to your Profile

Tap the Profile tab at the bottom of the screen.

3
Tap "Delete Account"

Scroll to the bottom of the Profile screen and tap Delete Account.

4
Confirm your decision

You will be asked to confirm. Once confirmed, your account deletion will be processed immediately.

Can't Access the App?

If you are unable to access the app, you can request account deletion by emailing us directly. Include your registered email address or phone number so we can locate your account.

Email us at:

Abikcare.hs@gmail.com

We will process your request within 7 business days and send a confirmation to your registered email address.

What Data Is Deleted or Kept

When your account is deleted, here is what happens to each type of data:

Data Type Action Retention Period
Name, email, phone number Deleted Immediately
Delivery addresses Deleted Immediately
Password (hashed) Deleted Immediately
Cart & saved items Deleted Immediately
Order records Kept Up to 7 years (legal/financial compliance)
Payment references Kept Up to 7 years (legal/financial compliance)

Order records and payment references are retained to meet Nigerian financial and tax regulations. These records do not include card details — AbikCare never stores card information.

After Deletion

  • You will be signed out of the AbikCare app immediately
  • You will not be able to sign in or recover your account
  • Any pending orders at time of deletion may still be fulfilled
  • You can create a new account in the future with the same email address
